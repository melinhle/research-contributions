# Making Research Contributions Visible

A static research project website for Caroline Monnin, Mê-Linh Lê, and Justin Fuhr, University of Manitoba Libraries.

## Upload to GitHub

1. Extract the supplied ZIP on your computer.
2. Open https://github.com/melinhle/research-contributions.
3. Choose **Add file → Upload files**.
4. Open the extracted folder and drag its contents (index.html, style.css, assets, presentations, README.md, and .nojekyll) into the upload area. Upload the contents, not the enclosing folder. If your computer hides .nojekyll, the site uses no special underscore folders and can still publish.
5. Choose **Commit changes**. Replace the existing README if prompted.
6. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
7. Under **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
8. Choose **main** and **/(root)**, then Save.
9. Once deployment completes, visit https://melinhle.github.io/research-contributions/.

## Preview and update

Open index.html in a browser for a local preview. Edit index.html for content, style.css for appearance, and replace assets as needed. No installation, build, external font, or JavaScript is required.

## Before publication

- Review all research summaries and author biographies for accuracy.
- Verify the sample-denominator explanation against your intended reporting. The adoption article reports both 2,590 screened records and 1,748 research/scholarly articles; its reported 6% uses 2,590, while 161/1,748 is about 9.2%.
- Check the use of the UM logo and supplied conference materials for your project context.
- Journal PDFs are linked through their DOIs rather than redistributed.
- Author portraits were retrieved from the authors' UM LibGuides profiles on 16 October 2026; source links are on the team cards.
- No blanket reuse license has been applied to papers, presentations, portraits, or institutional marks.
